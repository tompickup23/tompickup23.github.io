01 / The beginnings

A short history

Edward Stocks Massey was born in Brierfield in 1850. He made his fortune in Burnley’s brewing industry, becoming a partner in Massey’s Brewery alongside his brother Charles in 1883. Music was also part of his life: he served as choirmaster at St Luke’s Church in Brierfield.

When he died in 1909, he left £135,000 to benefit the people of Burnley. The bequest fund was established in 1910. The town’s corporation invested the gift, and trustees have maintained it over the years so it can continue to provide grants.

Its purpose spans education, science, learning, music and the arts. The Edward Stocks Massey Gallery at Towneley Hall and the Massey Music Library at Burnley Central Library are part of that legacy. Today, the fund supports local organisations and student scholarships.

Sources: Burnley Borough Council’s history of the bequest and Lancashire County Council’s account of the fund’s origins and purpose (PDF).
